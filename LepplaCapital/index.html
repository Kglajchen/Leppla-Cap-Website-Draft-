Overview of Our Structures

Layered Cash Management

Purpose
Keep surplus operating cash available when bills come due.
Bank role
Your bank stays the front door. Operating cash is scheduled around it.
Horizon
Next month through two years, scheduled to known needs.
How it is held
Treasuries timed to the cash-flow calendar.
Best when
Operating balances are idle and must stay available.

50 Basis Points · locked 2 years

Securities Investment Pyramid™

Purpose
Grow longer-horizon reserves under a written risk framework.
Bank role
A separate account. It does not replace your bank.
Horizon
Beyond the operating calendar.
How it is held
Six layers from Foundational through Direct; broader layers with written approval.
Best when
Reserves are surplus to near-term cash needs.

Clients use one structure or both. The fit depends on the mandate.
